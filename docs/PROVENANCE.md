Source range [794, 19404) frames, **0.018005–0.440000 s** at 44100 Hz; measured duration **0.421995 s**. Selected the first attack (strong rise at 0.02 s) and declining body (10 ms RMS bins decline from -3.4 dBFS at 0.11 s to about -25 dBFS at 0.42–0.44 s); excluded the later 6.30–6.50 s event. Mono averaging lost only **0.09 dB** of energy relative to native stereo, below the utility's 6 dB cancellation rejection threshold. Linear fades approximately **1.5 ms in / 15 ms out**, then peak normalization (gain approximately **-3.00 dB**); exported **16-bit PCM WAV, mono, 44100 Hz**, reopened sample peak **0.707947** (approximately **-3.00 dBFS**), no rail samples. No compression, limiting, pitch change, or time stretching. Source full-scale samples remain baked in: lowering gain does not repair possible source clipping.
